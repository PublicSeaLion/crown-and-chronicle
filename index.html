Prototype rules & controls

Goal: survive as many rounds as possible. You lose health when the enemy wins a battle.

  • Buy units from the shop. Bought units go to your bench.
  • Select a unit and click a green tile to deploy it. Your level controls your army-size cap.
  • Three identical 1★ units automatically merge into a 2★. Three 2★ units merge into a 3★.
  • Rerolls cost 2 gold. Buying 4 XP costs 4 gold. Interest pays +1 gold per 10 saved, up to +5.
  • Only units on the battlefield activate traits. Your King permanently changes your army's rules.
  • Combat is automatic. Units move, basic attack, gain mana, and cast their named ability at 100 mana.
